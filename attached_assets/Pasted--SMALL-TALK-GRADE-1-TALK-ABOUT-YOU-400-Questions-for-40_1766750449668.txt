# SMALL TALK - GRADE 1: TALK ABOUT YOU

## 400 Questions for 40 Weeks (10 per week)

-----

## WEEKS 1-4: BE VERBS - SELF INTRODUCTION & DESCRIPTIONS

### Week 1: Who Are You?

**Q1:** What is your name? How do you spell it?

- Grammar: Be verb (is)
- Starter: “My name is… I spell it…”
- Example: “My name is Yuki. I spell it Y-U-K-I.”

**Q2:** Where are you from? What city?

- Grammar: Be verb (am)
- Starter: “I am from…”
- Example: “I am from Osaka. It is a big city in Japan.”

**Q3:** How old are you? When is your birthday?

- Grammar: Be verb (am)
- Starter: “I am… years old. My birthday is…”
- Example: “I am 13 years old. My birthday is in April.”

**Q4:** Who is your best friend? Why?

- Grammar: Be verb (is)
- Starter: “My best friend is… He/She is…”
- Example: “My best friend is Taro. He is very kind and funny.”

**Q5:** What is your favorite color? Why?

- Grammar: Be verb (is)
- Starter: “My favorite color is… because…”
- Example: “My favorite color is blue because the sky is blue.”

**Q6:** Are you happy today? Why or why not?

- Grammar: Be verb (am)
- Starter: “Yes, I am happy because… / No, I am not happy because…”
- Example: “Yes, I am happy because today is Friday.”

**Q7:** What is your favorite food? Is it Japanese food?

- Grammar: Be verb (is)
- Starter: “My favorite food is… Yes, it is… / No, it is…”
- Example: “My favorite food is sushi. Yes, it is Japanese food.”

**Q8:** Who is your favorite teacher? Why?

- Grammar: Be verb (is)
- Starter: “My favorite teacher is… He/She is…”
- Example: “My favorite teacher is Mr. Tanaka. He is very patient.”

**Q9:** What is your favorite subject in school?

- Grammar: Be verb (is)
- Starter: “My favorite subject is…”
- Example: “My favorite subject is math because I like numbers.”

**Q10:** Are you a morning person or a night person?

- Grammar: Be verb (am)
- Starter: “I am a… person because…”
- Example: “I am a morning person because I have energy in the morning.”

## WEEKS 5-8: CAN/CAN’T - ABILITIES & POSSIBILITIES
