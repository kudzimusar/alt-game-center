## 400 Questions for 40 Weeks (10 per week)

## WEEKS 1-4: PRESENT PERFECT - EXPERIENCES & DURATION

### Week 1: Life Experiences

**Q1:** Have you ever been to another country? Where have you been?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “Yes, I have been to… / No, I have never been…”
- Example: “No, I have never been to another country, but I want to visit America.”

**Q2:** Have you ever tried a food that you did not like? What was it?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “Yes, I have tried… / No, I have never tried…”
- Example: “Yes, I have tried natto. I did not like it because the smell was too strong.”

**Q3:** Have you ever met a famous person? Who was it?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “Yes, I have met… / No, I have never met…”
- Example: “Yes, I have met a famous baseball player at an event. He was very tall.”

**Q4:** What is the most interesting thing you have ever done?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “The most interesting thing I have done is…”
- Example: “The most interesting thing I have done is climb Mount Fuji with my family.”

**Q5:** Have you ever won any competitions or awards? What for?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “Yes, I have won… / No, I have never won…”
- Example: “Yes, I have won a medal in a swim meet. I was very proud.”

**Q6:** Have you ever been really scared? What happened?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “Yes, I have been scared when…”
- Example: “Yes, I have been scared when I got lost in a big department store as a child.”

**Q7:** What is the best gift you have ever received?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “The best gift I have received is…”
- Example: “The best gift I have received is a bicycle from my parents for my birthday.”

**Q8:** Have you ever broken a bone or been seriously injured?

- Grammar: Present Perfect

- Example: “Yes, I have broken my arm when I fell off my bike two years ago.”

**Q9:** Have you ever stayed up all night? Why?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
- Starter: “Yes, I have stayed up… / No, I have never stayed up…”

**Q10:** What is the strangest thing you have ever seen?

- Grammar: Present Perfect
