# SMALL TALK - GRADE 2: WHAT IF?

## 400 Questions for 40 Weeks (10 per week)

-----

## WEEKS 1-4: PAST SIMPLE - MEMORIES & EXPERIENCES

### Week 1: Spring Vacation & Recent Past

**Q1:** What did you do during spring vacation? Where did you go?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “During spring vacation, I…”
- Example: “During spring vacation, I went to my grandparents’ house and relaxed.”

**Q2:** Did you travel anywhere last month? Where?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “Yes, I traveled to… / No, I did not travel…”
- Example: “Yes, I traveled to Kyoto with my family. We saw temples.”

**Q3:** What was the best meal you ate recently?

- Grammar: Past Simple (was)
- Starter: “The best meal was…”
- Example: “The best meal was sushi at a restaurant for my birthday.”

**Q4:** Who did you spend the most time with last week?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “Last week, I spent the most time with…”
- Example: “Last week, I spent the most time with my basketball team practicing.”

**Q5:** What did you learn in your classes last week?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “Last week, I learned…”
- Example: “Last week, I learned about photosynthesis in science class.”

**Q6:** Did you watch any good movies or shows recently?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “Yes, I watched… / No, I did not watch…”
- Example: “Yes, I watched a new anime called Spy Family. It was funny.”

**Q7:** What was the weather like yesterday?

- Grammar: Past Simple (was)
- Starter: “Yesterday, the weather was…”
- Example: “Yesterday, the weather was sunny and warm, perfect for playing outside.”

**Q8:** Did you have any tests last week? How did they go?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “Yes, I had… It went… / No, I did not have…”
- Example: “Yes, I had a math test. It went well because I studied hard.”

**Q9:** What did you eat for dinner last night?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “Last night, I ate…”
- Example: “Last night, I ate curry rice that my mother made.”

**Q10:** Did you do anything special last weekend? What?

- Grammar: Past Simple
- Starter: “Yes, I… / No, I did not…”
- Example: “Yes, I went shopping with friends and bought new shoes.”

- Comparing things, expressing preferences
- “Which is more important, money or happiness?”
- “What is the best thing about your school?”

### Weeks 25-28: INDIRECT QUESTIONS

- Polite inquiries
- “Can you tell me where the library is?”
- “Do you know what time it starts?”

### Weeks 29-32: PASSIVE VOICE

- Describing things and processes
- “What is your town known for?”
- “How is sushi made?”

### Weeks 33-36: MUST/HAVE TO

- Obligations and necessities
- “What do you have to do every day?”
- “What must we do to protect the environment?”

### Weeks 37-40: YEAR REVIEW (Mixed Grammar)

- Integration of all grammar points
- “Looking back, what did you learn this year and what will you do next year?”
