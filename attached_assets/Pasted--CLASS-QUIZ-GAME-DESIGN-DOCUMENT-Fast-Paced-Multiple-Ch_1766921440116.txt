# CLASS QUIZ - GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT

## Fast-Paced Multiple Choice Battles for JHS English

-----

## 🎮 GAME OVERVIEW

**Game Name:** Class Quiz

**Type:** Competitive Multiple Choice Quiz Battle

**Format:** Kahoot-style fast-paced team competition

**Duration:** 15-25 minutes per session

**Players:** Whole class (teams or individuals)

**Purpose:** Review grammar, vocabulary, and comprehension through exciting competition

-----

## 🎯 CORE GAME MECHANICS

### Game Flow

```
1. SETUP (2 minutes)
   ├─ Teacher selects quiz (grade + grammar point)
   ├─ Students join game with names/team names
   └─ Display game code for student devices (optional)

2. QUESTION ROUND (10-20 minutes)
   ├─ Display question + 4 answer choices
   ├─ Timer starts (10-30 seconds depending on difficulty)
   ├─ Students select answer on screen/device
   ├─ Timer ends, answers locked
   ├─ Reveal correct answer with explanation
   ├─ Show scoreboard update
   └─ Repeat for 10-20 questions

3. FINAL RESULTS (3 minutes)
   ├─ Display final leaderboard
   ├─ Crown winner(s)
   └─ Show statistics (accuracy, speed, etc.)
```

## 🏆 SCORING SYSTEM

### Points Calculation

**Base Points:**

- Correct Answer: 1,000 points
- Wrong Answer: 0 points

**Speed Bonus:**

- Answer in first 5 seconds: +500 bonus
- Answer in 6-10 seconds: +300 bonus
- Answer in 11-15 seconds: +100 bonus
- Answer after 15 seconds: No bonus

**Streak Bonus:**

- 3 correct in a row: +200 bonus
- 5 correct in a row: +500 bonus
- 10 correct in a row: +1,000 bonus

**Example:**
Student answers correctly in 7 seconds with 2-answer streak:

- Base: 1,000
- Speed: +300
- Total: 1,300 points

## 🎲 GAME MODES

### Mode 1: Team Battle (Default)

- Class divided into 4-6 teams
- Each team has a device or raises hand for answer
- Team points combined
- Encourages collaboration

### Mode 2: Individual Race

- Each student answers on own device
- Personal leaderboard
- Top 3 individuals win
- Good for 1:1 device environments

### Mode 3: Survival Mode

- Start with all students standing
- Wrong answer = sit down and eliminated
- Last person/team standing wins
- No devices needed - students show color cards

### Mode 4: Lightning Round

- 5-second timer per question
- 20 rapid-fire questions
- Tests quick thinking
- High energy finale

### Mode 5: Boss Battle

- Teams face increasingly difficult questions
- Each “level” has 3 questions
- Must get 2/3 correct to advance
- Reach final boss for bonus points

## 📚 QUESTION STRUCTURE

### Question Types

**Type 1: Fill in the Blank**

```
Question: "I ___ to school yesterday."
A) go
B) goes
C) went ✓
D) going

Grammar: Past Simple
Difficulty: Easy
Time: 15 seconds
```

**Type 2: Error Correction**

```
Question: "Find the mistake: She don't like apples."
A) She → He
B) don't → doesn't ✓
C) like → likes
D) apples → apple

Grammar: Simple Present (negative)
Difficulty: Medium
Time: 20 seconds
```

**Type 3: Best Answer**

```
Question: "Which sentence is correct?"
A) I am agree with you.
B) I agree with you. ✓
C) I agreeing with you.
D) I am agreeing with you.

Grammar: Stative verbs
Difficulty: Medium
Time: 20 seconds
```

**Type 4: Meaning Match**

```
Question: "I have lived here for 5 years." What does this mean?
A) I lived here 5 years ago and moved.
B) I started living here 5 years ago and still live here. ✓
C) I will live here for 5 years.
D) I want to live here for 5 years.

Grammar: Present Perfect (duration)
Difficulty: Hard
Time: 25 seconds
```

**Type 5: Word Order**

```
Question: "Put in correct order: went / yesterday / I / shopping"
A) I went shopping yesterday ✓
B) Yesterday I shopping went
C) I shopping went yesterday
D) Went I yesterday shopping

Grammar: Past Simple (sentence structure)
Difficulty: Easy
Time: 20 seconds
```

**Type 6: Vocabulary in Context**

```
Question: "The movie was ___. I fell asleep."
A) exciting
B) boring ✓
C) interesting
D) amazing

Grammar: Adjectives
Difficulty: Easy
Time: 15 seconds
```

## 📊 CONTENT ORGANIZATION

### Quiz Sets Structure

Each quiz set contains:

- **20 questions** (perfect for one class period)
- **Mix of difficulties**: 8 Easy, 8 Medium, 4 Hard
- **One grammar focus** or mixed review
- **Progressive difficulty** (easy → medium → hard → mixed)

### Annual Question Bank

**Per Grade Level:**

- 40 weeks × 2 quizzes per week = **80 quiz sets**
- 80 sets × 20 questions = **1,600 questions per grade**
- **Total: 4,800 questions across 3 grades**

**Organization by Grammar Point:**

**Grade 1 Example:**

```
Quiz Set G1-W01-A: Be Verbs (Introduction)
├─ Questions 1-8: Easy fill-in-blanks
├─ Questions 9-16: Medium error correction
└─ Questions 17-20: Hard mixed application

Quiz Set G1-W01-B: Be Verbs (Practice)
├─ Questions 1-8: Easy questions different from Set A
├─ Questions 9-16: Medium questions
└─ Questions 17-20: Hard questions
```

## 📈 DIFFICULTY PROGRESSION

### Within Each Quiz Set:

- Questions 1-8: Foundation (Easy) - Build confidence
- Questions 9-16: Application (Medium) - Test understanding
- Questions 17-20: Challenge (Hard) - Stretch thinking

### Across the Year:

- **Weeks 1-10:** More easy questions (60% easy, 30% medium, 10% hard)
- **Weeks 11-30:** Balanced mix (40% easy, 40% medium, 20% hard)
- **Weeks 31-40:** More challenging (30% easy, 40% medium, 30% hard)

### GRADE 1 - PRESENT PROGRESSIVE QUIZ

**Quiz Set G1-W14-B: Present Progressive - Actions Happening Now**

**EASY SECTION (Q1-8):**

**Q1:** Look! She ___ a book.

- A) read
- B) reads
- C) is reading ✓
- D) reading
- **Explanation:** Use “is reading” for actions happening right now.
- **Time:** 15 sec

**Q2:** They ___ soccer right now.

- A) play
- B) plays
- C) are playing ✓
- D) playing
- **Explanation:** Use “are playing” for plural subjects doing something now.
- **Time:** 15 sec

**Q3:** I ___ English at this moment.

- A) study
- B) am studying ✓
- C) studying
- D) studies
- **Explanation:** Use “am studying” with “I” for present progressive.
- **Time:** 15 sec

**Q4:** He ___ to music now.

- A) listen
- B) listens
- C) is listening ✓
- D) listening
- **Explanation:** Use “is listening” for he/she/it doing something now.
- **Time:** 15 sec

**Q5:** We ___ TV right now.

- A) watch
- B) watching
- C) are watching ✓
- D) watches
- **Explanation:** Use “are watching” for we/they doing something now.
- **Time:** 15 sec

**Q6:** The cat ___ on the bed.

- A) sleep
- B) sleeps
- C) is sleeping ✓
- D) sleeping
- **Explanation:** Use “is sleeping” for animals doing something now.

**Q7:** You ___ too fast!

- D) talking
- **Explanation:** Use “are talking” for you doing something now.

**Q8:** My mother ___ dinner now.

- A) cook
- B) cooks
- C) is cooking ✓
- D) cooking
- **Explanation:** Use “is cooking” for present progressive.

**MEDIUM SECTION (Q9-16):**

**Q9:** Which is CORRECT?

- A) I studying English now.
- B) I am studying English now. ✓
- C) I am study English now.
- D) I studying am English now.
- **Explanation:** Subject + am/is/are + verb-ing
- **Time:** 20 sec

**Q10:** Find the mistake: “She are eating lunch right now.”

- A) She → He
- B) are → is ✓
- C) eating → eat
- D) lunch → a lunch
- **Explanation:** “She” needs “is” not “are”
- **Time:** 20 sec

**Q11:** What is happening NOW? “It ___.”

- A) rain
- B) rains
- C) is raining ✓
- D) raining
- **Explanation:** Weather happening now uses present progressive
- **Time:** 20 sec

**Q12:** Change to present progressive: “They play basketball.”

- A) They playing basketball.
- B) They are play basketball.
- C) They are playing basketball. ✓
- D) They plays basketball.
- **Explanation:** Add “are” + change “play” to “playing”
- **Time:** 20 sec

**Q13:** Which shows action happening RIGHT NOW?

- A) I watch TV every day. (routine)
- B) I am watching TV. ✓ (now)
- C) I watched TV. (past)
- D) I will watch TV. (future)
- **Explanation:** Present progressive = happening now
- **Time:** 25 sec

**Q14:** “___ you doing your homework?” - “Yes, I am.”

- A) Do
- B) Are ✓
- C) Is
- D) Does
- **Explanation:** Questions use “Are you…?” for present progressive
- **Time:** 20 sec

**Q15:** Put in order: “eating / is / she / pizza”

- A) She is eating pizza. ✓
- B) She eating is pizza.
- C) Is she eating pizza?
- D) Eating she is pizza.
- **Explanation:** Subject + is + verb-ing + object
- **Time:** 20 sec

**Q16:** Find the mistake: “We are play soccer now.”

- A) We → They
- B) are → is
- C) play → playing ✓
- D) soccer → the soccer
- **Explanation:** Need “playing” not “play” after “are”
- **Time:** 20 sec

**HARD SECTION (Q17-20):**

**Q17:** Which is DIFFERENT from the others?

- A) I am studying now.

## 🎮 SPECIAL QUIZ FORMATS

### Format 1: SPEED ROUND (10 questions, 5 seconds each)

Ultra-fast review of basic concepts:

**Grade 2 - Past Simple Speed Round:**

- Q1: I ___ to school yesterday. (went)
- Q2: She ___ happy. (was)
- Q3: Did you ___? (go/eat/etc.)
- Q4: They ___ play soccer. (didn’t)
- Q5: He ___ a movie. (watched)
- Q6: We ___ not ready. (were)
- Q7: Did she ___? (come/study/etc.)
- Q8: It ___ cold. (was)
- Q9: I ___ my homework. (did)
- Q10: They ___ late. (arrived)

### Format 2: PICTURE ROUND

Questions with visual prompts:

**Example - Present Progressive:**

- Show image of person running
- Q: “What is he doing?”
- A) He run  B) He runs  C) He is running ✓  D) He running

### Format 3: ERROR HUNT

Find all errors in a paragraph:

**Example:**
“Yesterday, I go to the park. I am play soccer with my friends. We has a lot of fun. After that, I go home and eat dinner.”

**Errors:**

- go → went
- am play → played
- has → had
- go → went (second instance)

### Format 4: DIALOGUE COMPLETION

Complete a conversation:

**A:** “What ___ you do last weekend?”
**B:** “I ___ to the movies.”
**A:** “___ you enjoy it?”
**B:** “Yes, I ___. It ___ great!”

### Format 5: MIXED SKILLS CHALLENGE

Combines grammar, vocabulary, and comprehension:

- 5 grammar questions
- 5 vocabulary questions
- 5 reading comprehension questions
- 5 listening questions (if audio available)
